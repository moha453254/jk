Pro Cashier Mental Math Masterclass

Essential strategies used by high-speed cash register professionals

1. The "Count UP" Cashier Method

Never perform complex long subtraction in your head. Always add forward from the purchase price to the cash handed.

Example: Price = 3.35 JD | Customer gives 10.00 JD
• Step 1: Add 0.65 JD to reach 4.00 JD
• Step 2: Add 6.00 JD to reach 10.00 JD
• Result: Change is 6.65 JD!

2. The 1,000-Fils Complement Shortcut

To quickly calculate the fils part when subtracting from a clean dinar note:

Make the tens digit sum to 9 and the units digit sum to 10.
• Price has .35 JD fils?
• (9 - 3) = 6 & (10 - 5) = 5 ➔ .65 JD fils change!
• Then subtract 1 extra JD from the bill amount.

3. Managing "Awkward Cash" Payments

Smart shoppers hand extra coins to get rounded bills back (e.g., giving 20.35 JD for a 7.35 JD bill).

• Matching Fils: Ignore the .35 on both sides! Simply calculate 20 - 7 = 13.00 JD.
• Extra Coins: If price is 7.85 JD and customer gives 10.35 JD, subtract .35 from both: 10.00 JD - 7.50 JD = 2.50 JD change!

4. Grouping Banknotes for Drawer Picking

Always count change starting with the largest possible banknote down to coins to minimize physical movements.

To give 37.65 JD change:
Pick 20 JD ➔ 10 JD ➔ 5 JD ➔ Two 1 JD notes ➔ ½ JD coin ➔ 100 Fils ➔ 50 Fils.
